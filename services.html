Mobile Auto Detailing Services in San Antonio, TX

CeramiGuard Detailing offers a complete lineup of professional mobile services built to protect, restore, and transform your vehicle. Every service is performed on-site at your location—home, office, or wherever your car sits.

Ceramic Coating Packages

Our ceramic coating packages range from single-layer entry protection to multi-layer systems that deliver maximum hardness, gloss, and hydrophobic performance. Each package includes thorough surface preparation and paint decontamination to ensure a flawless bond. Packages are available for paint, wheels, glass, and trim.

Learn More About Ceramic Coating

Mobile Detailing

Whether you need a quick exterior wash and wax or a comprehensive interior-exterior detail, our mobile detailing packages cover it all. Services include hand wash, clay bar treatment, machine polish, interior vacuum and shampoo, leather cleaning and conditioning, glass cleaning, tire and trim dressing, and engine bay cleaning.

We bring all water, power, and equipment—you just point us to the car.

Schedule a Detail

Paint Correction

Paint correction is the process of removing surface imperfections—swirl marks, light scratches, water spots, and oxidation—through careful machine polishing. We offer single-stage, two-stage, and multi-stage correction depending on your paint's condition.

Paint correction is strongly recommended before ceramic coating to ensure the coating locks in a perfect finish.

Book Paint Correction

Dent Repair

Our paintless dent repair (PDR) service removes minor dents, dings, and hail damage without disturbing your vehicle's factory paint. PDR is faster, more affordable, and less invasive than traditional body work. Ideal for door dings, parking lot damage, and light hail impacts.

Get a Dent Repair Quote

Vinyl Wrap — Hoods, Roofs & Trunks

Want to add a custom look without a full wrap? We install precision-cut vinyl on hoods, roofs, and trunks in a wide range of finishes including gloss black, matte black, satin, carbon fiber, brushed metal, and color-shift films. Vinyl wraps also add a layer of paint protection against rock chips and scratches.
