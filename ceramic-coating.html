Professional Ceramic Coating in San Antonio, TX

CeramiGuard Detailing is San Antonio's go-to mobile ceramic coating provider. We apply professional-grade ceramic coatings on-site at your home or office, protecting your vehicle's paint with a durable, hydrophobic barrier that repels water, dirt, UV rays, and chemical contaminants.

What Is Ceramic Coating?

Ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that chemically bonds to your vehicle's clear coat, forming a semi-permanent layer of protection. Unlike traditional wax or sealant, which wears off in weeks, a properly applied ceramic coating lasts for years.

The result is a surface that's easier to clean, resistant to environmental damage, and delivers a head-turning gloss that doesn't fade.

Benefits of Ceramic Coating

💧

Hydrophobic Surface

Water and contaminants bead and slide off, making washes faster and easier.

☀️

UV Protection

Shields your paint from the intense South Texas sun, preventing oxidation and fading.

🧪

Chemical Resistance

Protects against bird droppings, tree sap, bug splatter, and road salt.

✨

Enhanced Gloss

Amplifies your paint's depth and clarity for a wet, mirror-like shine.

🛡

Scratch Resistance

Adds a hard sacrificial layer that reduces the risk of swirl marks and light scratches.

⏱

Long-Lasting

Professional coatings last 2–5+ years depending on the package, far outlasting wax or sealant.

Our Ceramic Coating Process

1

Full Decontamination

We hand wash, clay bar, and chemically decontaminate the paint to remove all bonded surface contaminants.

2

Paint Correction

We machine-polish the paint to remove swirl marks, scratches, and imperfections so the coating bonds to a flawless surface.

3

Panel Wipe

An IPA wipe-down removes all polishing oils, ensuring a clean, bare surface.

4

Coating Application

The ceramic coating is applied panel by panel, leveled, and allowed to flash before curing.

5

Curing & Inspection

The coating cures under controlled conditions. We perform a final inspection under LED lighting to confirm uniform coverage and bonding.
