Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. Here are the most common things our San Antonio customers ask about ceramic coating, mobile detailing, paint correction, and our other services.

Ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that bonds to your vehicle's clear coat, creating a durable, hydrophobic protective layer. Depending on the package, our ceramic coatings last anywhere from 1 to 5+ years with proper maintenance.

Absolutely. The South Texas sun, heat, and road conditions are brutal on automotive paint. Ceramic coating provides UV protection, chemical resistance, and makes your vehicle dramatically easier to keep clean—especially during pollen season and summer bug splatter.

Wax is a temporary surface protectant that wears off in a matter of weeks. Ceramic coating chemically bonds to the paint and lasts years. It offers significantly stronger protection against UV, chemicals, and scratches, and provides a deeper, more durable gloss.

Paint correction is a machine-polishing process that removes surface-level defects like swirl marks, light scratches, water spots, and oxidation. It restores your paint to a mirror-like finish and is an essential step before ceramic coating.
